Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version: YT Clipper runs on your own computer. There is no account, and the app sends nothing to us. The services you choose to connect (such as OpenRouter or Metricool) receive what they need to do their job, directly from your computer.

Who this covers

This policy covers the website ytclipper.xyz and the YT Clipper desktop app for Windows, both made by Hasbiyallahu Jafaru ("we", "us"). It explains what information is handled, by whom, and why.

The website

  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising. We do not set tracking cookies or run analytics scripts on this site.
  • Hosting. The site is served by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes your IP address and basic request details (browser, time, page) to deliver pages and keep the service secure.
  • The download check. When you press Download, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the request comes from a person. To do this Cloudflare processes signals such as your IP address and browser characteristics. It does not ask you to identify yourself. The installer is then delivered by Cloudflare.

The desktop app

The app works on your computer. Your projects, renders, clips, settings and history are stored in your Windows user folder and never uploaded to us. The app has no telemetry and does not report usage to us.

The API keys and sign-in tokens you add are encrypted with Windows' built-in protection (DPAPI) and kept on your computer.

Services you choose to connect

Some features use outside services. They only run when you set them up, and the app talks to them directly from your computer. Each service handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Children

YT Clipper is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can disconnect any service in the app's Settings, delete any project or file from inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove it. Your data folder stays on your computer until you delete it. Because we do not hold your data, requests about data held by a connected service go to that service.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact options at hasbiyallahu.xyz.
